The Practice

Dermatology practiced as an exacting science and an intimate craft.

Skin is not a superficial veneer — it is our primary immunological barrier, a living organ of sensory communication, and the canvas of personal vitality. At Lustre Lab, treatment begins with dermoscopic diagnostic certainty and proceeds through evidence-based procedural protocols.

Whether performing diagnostic skin biopsies, scar revision surgery, or complex facial rejuvenation with biostimulators and autologous fat, Dr. Sindhu Muthuraja maintains an unwavering clinical standard: ethical, conservative, and individualized to each patient's anatomical harmony.

Every treatment is approached through careful clinical assessment, an understanding of individual anatomy, and a considered balance between medical necessity and aesthetic intent. The objective is not unnecessary intervention, but thoughtful, evidence-led care that respects the integrity and long-term health of the skin.
